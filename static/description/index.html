Expenses & Cash Control

المصروفات ومراقبة النقد

Record every expense in seconds, keep the bills you still owe, and close every POS day with a clean cash drawer.

Expenses

  • Paid now from the cash box or bank: a posted journal entry is created automatically.
  • To pay later: recorded as a real vendor bill that waits for payment.
  • Receipts: attach photos or PDFs; they are copied to the journal entry / bill.
  • Edit anytime: the accounting entry is updated in place, without sequence gaps.

POS Cash Control

  • Categorized Cash In / Out right in the POS popup, posted on the category account.
  • Daily reconciliation of uncategorized cash in / out recorded by cashiers.
  • Cash differences (loss / gain) detected on closing and reclassified in one click.
  • Session review: opening, cash in/out, expected, counted and difference on one screen.
